# Embodied-Intelligence Power Industrial Control Systems (2026)

- **Source:** [IEEE/CAA JAS, DOI 10.1109/JAS.2026.125846](https://doi.org/10.1109/JAS.2026.125846)
- **Role:** A short architecture/research-recommendation article that frames embodied intelligence for power industrial control as a systems problem rather than a single model problem.
- **Knowledge-base conclusion:** The research object must make perception, cognition/planning, execution/control and physical feedback explicit. A useful paper should name the physical or simulated embodiment, failure containment path and human authority.
- **Open questions:** world-model fidelity, multi-timescale decision-making, communication failures, sim-to-real/HIL validation, cyber security and assurance cases.
- **Use in this project:** This card supplies the definition and research checklist; the benchmark cards supply the executable evaluation environments.
